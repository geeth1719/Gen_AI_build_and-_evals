Here is a complete, modular pipeline for a Guardrail & Model Routing Engine.

It works in two sequential stages:

Guardrails Layer: Checks for prompt injection, safety violations, and masks PII (Emails, Phone numbers, SSNs) before any model touches the input.

Dynamic Router: Calculates a Complexity & Intent Score based on token length, contextual depth, and task requirements to route simple tasks to a Small Language Model (SLM) and complex reasoning to a Large Language Model (LLM).

Pipeline Architecture
